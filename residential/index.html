Clear Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Before we pick up a wrench, we find out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check your system's age and tonnage, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. After that you get a straightforward recommendation and the full price up front, whether the job is swapping a weak capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons.

The problem with HVAC failures is that most of them develop where you can't see them. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil bleeds off refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down gradually, and a weak capacitor lasts right up until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. If your bills are climbing, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, have the system checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most air conditioners and furnaces last 12 to 18 years, and a planned replacement is always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap during a heat wave or cold snap. Our technicians know the climate demands common to Columbus and what they do to equipment over time, so we can tell you honestly whether your system has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service prevents failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still inexpensive to fix. At Columbus HVAC, every plan is built around your home's specific equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check across the duct system.

Most homes do best with two visits a year: a springtime tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating. The schedule flexes with the age of your equipment, how hard your household works it, and local conditions like dust and pollen, which clog condenser coils and push up energy use fast.

Consider maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up helps keep the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most households, a plan costs far less than replacing the system ahead of schedule.

Sized for Your Home

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

An undersized system runs nonstop and never catches up on the hottest days, while an oversized one short-cycles, wastes energy, and leaves the house humid. Columbus HVAC sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual home. That means a proper load calculation that accounts for square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout, rather than simply matching the tonnage of the old unit. For ductless mini-splits, it means matching head capacity to each zone so every room holds its setpoint.

We walk you through the options for every home, especially growing families with rising comfort demands. A single-stage system that served a small household rarely satisfies a finished basement and two added bedrooms, and a modern two-stage or variable-speed heat pump can hold tighter temperatures while cutting energy use dramatically. If you're weighing furnace-and-AC against a heat pump, or ducted against ductless, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: SEER2 efficiency ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting requirements, and upfront installation work, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Common Problems

The HVAC Repairs Columbus Homes Need Most

The majority of repair calls we get in Columbus come down to a handful of familiar problems. The air conditioner blows warm air. The furnace won't ignite or keeps short-cycling. Ice builds up on the evaporator coil, the condenser hums without starting, or a mini-split starts flashing error codes. Add refrigerant leaks, clogged drains, and forgetful thermostats, and you have the full list. We fix every one of them, always targeting the component that actually failed.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A weak capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. We come prepared to replace capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
