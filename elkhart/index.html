Two common situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can overflow the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or produces a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Elkhart

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
